## What's Next?

The category is fully built and the AI layer is being absorbed by incumbents this year. Realistic narrow wedges:

- **Maintenance-vendor side, not landlord side.** All incumbents own the landlord; nobody owns the plumber / electrician / handyman who services 30 small landlords. Marketplace position with AI dispatch.
- **Multi-state portfolio compliance.** Landlords with units in 3+ states currently juggle state-specific lease rules, late-fee caps, eviction rules. Generic SaaS papers over this; an AI compliance-engine layer is unowned.
- **Mom-and-pop accounting + tax filing.** Stessa is closest but most landlords still hand off to CPAs. End-to-end "K-1 generation + Schedule E filing for the small landlord" is unclaimed.
- **Section 8 / housing-voucher-specific.** Federal program with brutal paperwork; specific tools for landlords accepting vouchers don't exist.
- **Reading:** If you're not willing to enter a sub-vertical, do not enter this lane. The generic "AI property manager for small landlords" was just funded by YC at Brickwise.
