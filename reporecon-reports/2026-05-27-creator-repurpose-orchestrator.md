## What's Next?

This is the most contested category in the five-idea round. Realistic narrow wedges:

- **Vertical-creator focus** — financial-advisor creators (compliance-checked repurposing for FINRA), medical creators (FDA-safe language), real-estate agents (MLS-compliant). Generic tools can't navigate domain rules.
- **Non-English-first** — most incumbents are English-centric. Hindi / Mandarin / Spanish / Arabic creator markets are large and underserved.
- **Long-running campaigns** — incumbents do one-shot conversions; nobody owns "9-month book launch repurposing arc with calendar-driven pacing."
- **Compliance-grade fact-check** — generic AI repurposing has high hallucination tolerance. Compliance/legal/medical writers need provable citation linkage; no incumbent offers this.
- **Recommendation:** do not enter the generic "repurpose for everyone" lane. The buyer is over-served and price-sensitive. Pick a vertical or a moat (compliance, multilingual, long-cycle) or step away.
