## What's Next?

Direct entry is not advisable. Possible narrow angles:
- **Non-US jurisdictions** — incumbents are heavily US-centric. UK/Canada/Australia probate has distinct law and lighter SaaS.
- **Religious / cultural-specific probate flows** — orthodox Jewish, Sharia inheritance, Hindu joint-family laws have unique workflows not covered by generic US-shaped tools.
- **Attorney-channel only** — Estateably/AdviKit own this, but the multi-state mid-firm middle is contested rather than saturated.
- **Digital-only estates** — crypto/keys/passwords/online-account closure: GoodTrust is small here; could be a sharper wedge.
- **Caregiver-to-executor handoff** — combine with idea #4 (aging-parent caregiver). The same family member often becomes the executor; nobody owns the transition.
